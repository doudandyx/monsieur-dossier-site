The French rental file, one question at a time.

Each guide answers one question, cites the law or the official source, and shows the date it was last updated.

What documents can a landlord ask for when you rent in France?

To rent a home in France, a landlord can ask for four kinds of documents: identity, current address, occupation and income. The list is set by decree.

What documents is a French landlord not allowed to ask for?

A French landlord cannot require any document outside the list set by decree: no bank statements, no health card, no criminal record, and no money to reserve the home.

Do I have to give my bank details (RIB) to rent in France?

No. Your bank details (RIB) are not on the list of documents a French landlord can require before the lease is signed, and the CNIL names bank statements as off limits.

Can I rent a home in France without a guarantor?

Yes. French law does not require a guarantor, and Visale, a free guarantee from Action Logement, can stand in for one if you are aged up to 30 or a recent hire.

How do I put together a French rental file when I arrive from abroad?

The same four kinds of documents, with what you have: passport or residence permit, employment contract, a foreign tax notice, all translated into French.

What should the watermark on my French rental documents say?

Write who the copy is for, what it is for and the date: that is the advice of Cybermalveillance.gouv.fr, the French government’s service, to keep a copy from being reused.

What can I hide on my French rental documents?

No law settles it. Our reading: hide what serves neither to assess your file nor to verify the document, such as your IBAN or social security number. Never your tax number.

How do I redact a PDF on a Mac without uploading it?

With Preview, the app that comes with macOS: according to Apple, the Redaction Selection tool removes text from view, and the redaction becomes permanent once you close the document.

I sent my rental documents to a scammer in France: what do I do?

Keep the evidence, tell your bank, report the listing and file a complaint. If an identity document went out, also file a “main courante” and watch your accounts.
