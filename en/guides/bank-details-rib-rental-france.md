# Do I have to give my bank details (RIB) to rent in France?

> No, you do not have to give your bank details to apply for a rental in France. The RIB, the slip that shows your IBAN, is not on the list of documents a landlord can require from an applicant, set by [décret n° 2015-1437](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/id/JORFTEXT000031444493). We infer this from the list: no official page we read names the RIB. The [CNIL](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/location-dun-bien-immobilier-quels-justificatifs), for its part, names bank statements among the documents that cannot be asked for. Once the lease is signed, you give your RIB if you choose direct debit, which the [law](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/id/JORFTEXT000000509310/) forbids forcing on you.

## What does a landlord use a RIB for?

To set up a direct debit for the rent. That need only exists once the lease is signed. Before that, a RIB says nothing about your income: for that, the [decree](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/id/JORFTEXT000031444493) lists proof of income, such as your payslips or your tax notice.

## Can a landlord impose direct debit?

No. Article 4 of the [law of 6 July 1989](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/id/JORFTEXT000000509310/) treats as void any clause that imposes automatic direct debit from the tenant’s account as the way to pay the rent. [Service-public.gouv.fr](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F1686) lists it among the clauses forbidden in a lease. You can choose direct debit, but it cannot be forced on you.

## Can a landlord ask for bank statements?

No. The [CNIL](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/location-dun-bien-immobilier-quels-justificatifs), the French data protection authority, names bank statements among the documents that agencies and landlords alike cannot ask for, along with the Carte Vitale health card and a criminal record extract. A letter from your bank about how your account is run is not on the [decree](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/id/JORFTEXT000031444493)’s list either.

## My IBAN shows on another document: what should I do?

A payslip sometimes shows the IBAN of the account your salary is paid into. In our view, the landlord does not need it to assess your file. You can hide it before you send the copy: see [what you can hide on your documents](https://monsieurdossier.com/en/guides/what-to-hide-rental-documents-france.html).

## What should you answer if you are asked for your RIB anyway?

- Point out that the RIB is not among the documents listed in [décret n° 2015-1437](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/id/JORFTEXT000031444493).
- Offer to hand it over when the lease is signed, if you choose direct debit.
- If you are asked for your papers before any viewing, be careful: [service-public.gouv.fr](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F34092) lists a request for official documents before the viewing, an identity card for example, among the signs of a fake listing. See [what to do if your file went to a scammer](https://monsieurdossier.com/en/guides/rental-documents-sent-to-scammer-france.html).
