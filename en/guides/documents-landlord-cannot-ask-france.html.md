# What documents is a French landlord not allowed to ask for?

> A landlord or agency in France is not allowed to require any document outside the list set by [décret n° 2015-1437](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/id/JORFTEXT000031444493) of 5 November 2015. The [CNIL](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/location-dun-bien-immobilier-quels-justificatifs), the French data protection authority, names among the documents you cannot be asked for: bank statements, the Carte Vitale health card, a criminal record extract, the family record book, a marriage contract and a divorce judgment. No money can be asked to reserve the home either. According to [service-public.gouv.fr](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F1169), the landlord or agency risks a fine of up to €3,000, or €15,000 for a legal entity.

## Why are these documents forbidden?

Because the list of documents a landlord can require is set by law. The [law of 6 July 1989](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/id/JORFTEXT000000509310/) refers to a [decree](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/id/JORFTEXT000031444493), which names the documents allowed for the applicant and for the guarantor. [Service-public.gouv.fr](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F1169) draws the consequence: a landlord who demands a document outside the list risks a fine. The full list is in the guide on [the documents a landlord can ask for](https://monsieurdossier.com/en/guides/documents-landlord-can-ask-france.html).

## Which examples does the CNIL give?

The [CNIL](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/location-dun-bien-immobilier-quels-justificatifs) writes that estate agencies and landlords alike cannot ask for certain documents, such as:

- a copy of the family record book (livret de famille)
- the Carte Vitale health insurance card
- bank statements
- a marriage contract
- a divorce judgment
- a criminal record extract

> These are examples: the CNIL writes “such as”. The general rule is the decree’s. What is not on the list cannot be required.

## Which other documents are not on the list?

Other documents are sometimes asked for. None of the four below is in the [decree](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/id/JORFTEXT000031444493), so none can be required. This is our reading of the list: the official pages we read do not name them one by one.

- your bank details (RIB), or a direct-debit authorisation signed in advance
- a letter from your bank about how your account is run
- medical records
- an identity photo, on top of the one on your identity document

## Can you be asked for money before the lease is signed?

No, according to [service-public.gouv.fr](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F1169): the landlord or agency is forbidden to ask for money in return for reserving the home, and no sum can be asked for before the lease is signed. The security deposit is paid at signing, says its [page on the security deposit](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F31269). The [law](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/id/JORFTEXT000000509310/) caps it at one month of rent excluding charges for an unfurnished home and two months for a furnished one.

## What does the landlord risk?

[Article 22-2 of the law](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/id/JORFTEXT000000509310/) provides for an administrative fine, imposed by the prefect of the département: at most €3,000 for an individual and €15,000 for a legal entity. The amount depends on how serious the facts are, and the fine can no longer be imposed more than a year after they were recorded. The [decree](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/id/JORFTEXT000031444493) adds that the prefect first informs the landlord, who has two months to respond.

## What should you do if you are asked for a forbidden document?

1. State the rule in writing: the list of documents is set by [décret n° 2015-1437](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/loda/id/JORFTEXT000031444493).
2. Offer the allowed document that meets the same need, for example your tax notice instead of a bank statement.
3. Keep a record of the request: email, message, screenshot of the listing.
4. If the request stands, ask the ADIL, the housing information agency of your département, which [service-public.gouv.fr](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F1169) gives as a contact. You can also write to the prefect of your département, since the prefect is the one who imposes the fine.

> These four steps are our advice. No official page we read describes how to report an illegal request.
