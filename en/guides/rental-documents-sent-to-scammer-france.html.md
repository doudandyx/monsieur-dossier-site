# I sent my rental documents to a scammer in France: what do I do?

> If you sent your French rental file to a fake landlord, [Cybermalveillance.gouv.fr](https://www.cybermalveillance.gouv.fr/tous-nos-contenus/fiches-reflexes/arnaques-location-immobiliere), the French government’s help service for victims, recommends keeping all the evidence, telling your bank, reporting the listing to the platform that carries it, then filing a complaint. If you paid money, ask your bank to stop the payment as fast as you can. If you sent a copy of an identity document, also file a “main courante”, a dated police record. Your documents can be used to open an account or take out a loan in your name: the [Banque de France](https://www.banque-france.fr/fr/a-votre-service/particuliers/agir-usurpation-identite) explains how to check.

## What should you do right away?

[Cybermalveillance.gouv.fr](https://www.cybermalveillance.gouv.fr/tous-nos-contenus/fiches-reflexes/arnaques-location-immobiliere), the French government’s help service for victims, gives these steps, in this order:

1. **Keep the evidence**: screenshots of the listing, messages, bank statements.
2. **Tell your bank** and check the transactions on your account regularly.
3. **If you paid money**, by transfer, cheque or card, ask your bank to stop the payment as fast as you can, to try to block the transaction.
4. **Report the listing** to the platform that published it.
5. **File a complaint**, with all your evidence.
6. **If you sent a copy of an identity document, file a “main courante”** at a police station or a gendarmerie. It puts the facts on record for later action.

## Where can you file a complaint or get advice?

- **THESEE**, the online service for reporting an internet scam, and for filing a complaint if you are an adult. According to [service-public.gouv.fr](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/R47990), two conditions must both be met: you never met the scammer in person, and they asked you for money, whether or not you paid. A report is not a complaint.
- **The [online complaint service](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/R19620)** of the Interior Ministry, for an offence against property, fraud included, when the offender is unknown. A police officer or gendarme may ask you to come in and complete your statement.
- **A police station or a gendarmerie**, or a letter to the public prosecutor.
- **France Victimes**, on 116 006. The call is free, and the association can support you through the steps ([Cybermalveillance.gouv.fr](https://www.cybermalveillance.gouv.fr/tous-nos-contenus/fiches-reflexes/arnaques-location-immobiliere)).
- **Info Escroqueries**, on 0 805 805 817. The call is free, Monday to Friday from 9 am to 6.30 pm ([Cybermalveillance.gouv.fr](https://www.cybermalveillance.gouv.fr/tous-nos-contenus/fiches-reflexes/arnaques-location-immobiliere)).
- **[17Cyber](https://17cyber.gouv.fr)**, the online help service of the police, the gendarmerie and Cybermalveillance.gouv.fr.

According to [service-public.gouv.fr](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F34092), fraud is punished by five years in prison and a €375,000 fine, and you have six years to file a complaint, counted from the last act of fraud.

## How do you protect yourself against identity theft?

The [Banque de France](https://www.banque-france.fr/fr/a-votre-service/particuliers/agir-usurpation-identite) and [Cybermalveillance.gouv.fr](https://www.cybermalveillance.gouv.fr/tous-nos-contenus/fiches-reflexes/usurpation-identite-que-faire) advise you to:

- tell all your banks, and your employer if you sent payslips
- ask for the list of accounts opened in your name in the Ficoba register, through the secure messaging in your impots.gouv.fr account or by post to your tax office
- check with the Banque de France that you are not listed in its register of cheque incidents (FCC) or its register of loan repayment incidents (FICP), and repeat the check from time to time. For more information, the Banque de France gives the number 34 14
- file a complaint for each use of your identity. According to Cybermalveillance.gouv.fr, identity theft carries up to one year in prison and a €15,000 fine
- have an identity document that scammers used cancelled and renewed

[Service-public.gouv.fr](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F37944) separates two cases. If you only suspect identity theft, you can file a “main courante”. As soon as your identity is used without your knowledge, you can file a complaint.

## Should you tell your guarantor?

Yes, in our view, if you also sent their documents. The official pages we read do not mention it. Their documents face the same risk as yours, and they can take the same steps in their own name.

## How do you recognise a fake listing?

[Service-public.gouv.fr](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F34092) lists these signs:

- a very low price for the location, size or standard of the home
- photos that appear in another listing
- a landlord who behaves oddly, for example by refusing to meet you
- a request for money before the lease is signed
- a request for official documents, an identity card for example, before the viewing

[Cybermalveillance.gouv.fr](https://www.cybermalveillance.gouv.fr/tous-nos-contenus/fiches-reflexes/arnaques-location-immobiliere) adds three pieces of advice. Refuse any payment to an account abroad, in cash or through a money transfer service. Wait until you receive the keys to pay the security deposit and the first rent, by bank transfer or by cheque. If in any doubt, ask the landlord for a copy of their property tax notice, to make sure the home is really theirs.

## How do you protect the next copies you send?

[Cybermalveillance.gouv.fr](https://www.cybermalveillance.gouv.fr/tous-nos-contenus/fiches-reflexes/arnaques-location-immobiliere) recommends marking each copy with the reason for sending it, the date and the recipient, and handing documents over in person when you can. You can also hide what the recipient does not need. See [what the watermark should say](https://monsieurdossier.com/en/guides/watermark-text-rental-documents-france.html) and [what you can hide on your documents](https://monsieurdossier.com/en/guides/what-to-hide-rental-documents-france.html).
