What should the watermark on my French rental documents say?

Write three things in the watermark on your French rental documents: who the copy is for, what it is for, and the date. This is the advice of Cybermalveillance.gouv.fr, the French government’s cybersecurity help service, which recommends writing on each copy the reason for sending it, the date and the recipient, so that it cannot be reused for fraud. An example, written by us: “Copy – Rental application 12 rue des Lilas – For Agence Saône only – 11/2026”.

Updated on 24 November 2026

Why name the recipient and date the copy?

Cybermalveillance.gouv.fr recommends it to keep your documents from being reused for fraud. Our explanation: a general phrase does not say where the copy came from. FiligraneFacile, the government’s watermarking service, suggests “Document exclusivement destiné à la location immobilière”, meaning for property rental only. Such a phrase limits the use, but it would still hold for another rental. With the name of the agency or landlord and the date, each copy is tied to a single send.

Which wording should you use?

No official page gives a wording with recipient and date. The three examples below are ours: one short, readable line with the recipient, the purpose and the month.

  • for an agency: “Copy – Rental application 12 rue des Lilas – For Agence Saône only – 11/2026”
  • for a private landlord: “Copy – Rental 5 place Carnot – For Mr Durand only – 11/2026”
  • in French: “Copie – Dossier location 12 rue des Lilas – Usage exclusif Agence Saône – 11/2026”

FiligraneFacile limits the text to 100 characters. All three examples fit within that limit.

Where should the watermark go?

Cybermalveillance.gouv.fr speaks of writing the mention “over” the copy. Our advice goes the same way: across the whole page, repeated, over the text, because a mention placed in a margin can be cropped out. The document must stay readable. Do not cover the 2D-Doc code on a tax notice: according to impots.gouv.fr, it is used to check that the notice has not been altered.

Which tool can add a watermark?

  • FiligraneFacile, the government’s online service. You upload a jpg, png, heic or pdf file, type the text and get a PDF back. The service says it erases the originals from its servers once the file is generated, and the watermarked file after its first download, or after one day. The CNIL, the French data protection authority, presents it as a way to protect sensitive documents before sending them.
  • DossierFacile, the government’s service for rental files. Cybermalveillance.gouv.fr names it, with FiligraneFacile, among the official sites that help secure a file or mark copies with a watermark.
  • France Identité, the government’s app. It creates a single-use proof of identity: a PDF signed by the Interior Ministry, for which you set a recipient, a duration and a purpose, in place of a photocopy of your identity document. The conditions for using it are on its site.

Is a watermark enough to protect a copy?

No, in our view. A watermark deters misuse and shows where a copy came from, but image retouching can fade or remove it. It does not replace hiding the numbers the landlord does not need: see what you can hide on your documents.

Can a watermarked document be refused?

Yes, it can happen. FiligraneFacile says so itself: adding a watermark is good practice, but some organisations, such as banks or public services, may need a clean document. Always keep your originals.
