## Feedback or a bug? Write to us.

Monsieur Dossier is a young app: your feedback decides what comes next. Tell us what helped, what got in your way, what is missing.

**Never send your documents.** No ID, no payslip, no screenshot of a document, not even as an attachment. Just describe the problem in words.

Write to us at [contact@monsieurdossier.com](mailto:contact@monsieurdossier.com)

For a bug: what you were doing, what you expected, what happened, and your macOS version. If the button opens nothing, copy the address and write from your usual mailbox.
