With DossierFacile

It works alongside DossierFacile, not against it.

DossierFacile, the French government’s free service, verifies your file and shares it by link. Monsieur Dossier protects the files you send yourself, when the link isn’t accepted or you’re asked for attachments.

How to use them together

1. Get verified

Create your DossierFacile and share its link whenever the landlord accepts it.

2. Protect the rest

Asked for files by email or on a portal? Prepare them with Monsieur Dossier.

3. Keep what’s checkable

Never hide your tax number, the tax notice reference, QR codes or your photo.

Monsieur Dossier is an independent app. It is not a government service, is not affiliated with DossierFacile, and does not check that your documents are genuine.
