# Monsieur Dossier and DossierFacile: who does what

> Monsieur Dossier works alongside DossierFacile, the French government’s service: what each one does, and how to use them together.

Language: English · [Version française](https://monsieurdossier.com/dossierfacile.html.md) · [Web page](https://monsieurdossier.com/en/dossierfacile.html) · Updated: 23 November 2026

## It works alongside DossierFacile, not against it.

[DossierFacile](https://www.dossierfacile.logement.gouv.fr/), the French government’s free service, verifies your file and shares it by link. Monsieur Dossier protects the files you send yourself, when the link isn’t accepted or you’re asked for attachments.

| | DossierFacile | Monsieur Dossier |
|---|---|---|
| Its job | Verify your file and share it | Prepare and protect your files |
| Your documents | Uploaded to government servers, with an account | Stay on your Mac, no account |
| Verification | Yes: a government “verified” label | No: nothing is certified |
| Sharing | One revocable link per recipient | Files you attach yourself |
| Hidden data | None: complete documents required | IBAN, bank codes, social security no., MRZ, plus your own areas |
| Watermark | Fixed wording, no name or date | Recipient, address and date |
| Language | French | English and French |

## How to use them together

1. **Get verified.** Create your DossierFacile and share its link whenever the landlord accepts it.
2. **Protect the rest.** Asked for files by email or on a portal? Prepare them with Monsieur Dossier.
3. **Keep what’s checkable.** Never hide your tax number, the tax notice reference, QR codes or your photo.

Monsieur Dossier is an independent app. It is not a government service, is not affiliated with DossierFacile, and does not check that your documents are genuine.
